# Engagement Types

Cuesoft is deliberately lean. Work gets done by three kinds of people, and
a signed agreement governs each. This page is the map; the agreement you
signed is the territory.

| Engagement                  | Who                                                                                              | Governing document                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Founders**                | The four leaders who run the divisions and teach at CueTA™: see [leadership](../../leadership/). |                                                                      |
| **Independent contractors** | Specialists engaged for defined scopes of work (engineering, design, and more).                  | Independent Contractor Agreement: see [contractors](../contractors/) |
| **CueLABS™ interns**        | Engineers and researchers in the open-source internship programme.                               | Internship Agreement: see [internships](../internships/)             |

## What this means in practice

- **No employment relationship.** Contractors and interns are not employees,
  agents or representatives of Cuesoft, and nothing in the handbook creates
  an employment relationship. Nobody may enter contracts or incur
  liabilities on Cuesoft's behalf except the founders.
- **Own tools.** Contractors and interns provide their own equipment and
  work environment (see [BYOD](../../policies/byod/)).
- **Own taxes.** Contractors are solely responsible for their taxes and
  statutory payments on compensation received.
- **Confidentiality and IP travel with the agreement.** Every engagement
  carries confidentiality obligations and intellectual-property assignment:
  see the [confidentiality](../../policies/confidentiality/) and
  [intellectual property](../../policies/intellectual-property/) policies.
- **The pathway is real, never promised.** Interns and academy learners can
  go forward through CueHIRE™ into client work; nobody at Cuesoft promises
  anyone employment, a placement or a salary.

## More detail

### Who this covers

The founders, independent contractors and CueLABS™ interns, as in the table
above, and anyone else acting for Cuesoft Nigeria Limited or Cuesoft Inc.
Each kind of engagement has a page of its own: [contractors](../contractors/)
and [internships](../internships/).

### No employment, no authority

- Nothing in this handbook or in how the work is done creates employment,
  partnership, joint venture or agency. No employee-type entitlement (pay
  for time away, benefits, notice or severance) arises from an engagement
  unless the signed agreement says so.
- Only the founders may sign, promise or pay anything on Cuesoft's behalf.
  If someone tells you otherwise, ask a founder.
- Founders follow the same policies as everyone else.

### Conflicts of interest and outside work

Declare a conflict of interest to a founder before you take on the conflicting
work, and never use Cuesoft or client information, accounts or time for
outside work: see [contractors](../contractors/). Interns do the same.

### Equipment

Contractors and interns bring their own equipment under the
[BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/). Cuesoft property stays Cuesoft's under
[company assets](../company-assets/).

### Ending an engagement

Each agreement says how it ends. On the end of any engagement, access is
revoked, work and materials are handed over, property is returned, and
confidentiality and IP assignment continue: see
[onboarding and offboarding](../onboarding-offboarding/). Severe misconduct
ends an engagement immediately: see [violations](../violation/).

### Concerns, records, law and changes

Report a concern as [the policies page](../../policies/#reporting-a-concern)
says. Personal details and engagement records follow the
[personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) and are kept
as the privacy policy's [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/)
says. Local law applies on top of this page, the signed agreement prevails
where they differ, and this page changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../../policies/#changing-a-policy).
