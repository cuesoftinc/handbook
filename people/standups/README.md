# Standups

Teams run a daily standup during active sprints: what you finished since
the last one, what you are doing next, and what is blocking you.

- **Interns** attend the standups of their programme's sprints — the
  ceremony is part of the training. **Contractors** attend the ceremonies
  their signed scope includes, and no others.
- If you cannot make one, post your update asynchronously in your team
  channel (Slack for contractors, Discord for interns) **before** the
  standup time — an async update counts; silence does not.
- Keep it to updates and blockers. Anything that needs discussion gets its
  own thread or call with the people it concerns.
- Going silent on a committed ceremony is a commitment problem — see
  [work ethic](../../general/work-ethic/) and [violations](../violation/).
