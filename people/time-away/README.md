# Time Away

We are deliverable-based, not clock-based, so there is no leave ledger:
there is communication.

- **Contractors** manage their own schedules. What matters is the agreed
  scope and milestones; plan your time away so commitments hold, and tell
  the people your absence affects before it starts.
- **Interns** coordinate time away with their supervisor ahead of time, so
  sprint planning can account for it. Reasonable time away is never held
  against anyone: unannounced disappearance during committed work is a
  different matter (see [work ethic](../../general/work-ethic/)).
- **During client engagements**, absences that affect the client's sprint
  or SLA must be flagged to the engagement lead early enough to cover them:
  see [client engagements](../../engineering/clients/).

Rest is part of doing excellent work. Take it, plan it, communicate it.

## More detail

### Who this applies to

Contractors and interns, and the founders for their own planning. It covers
planned and unplanned absence of any kind: rest, illness, family or personal
matters, study, religious observance, public holidays and travel.

### What time away is, and is not

- Time away is communicated, not approved against a ledger. Nothing here
  creates a leave entitlement, an accrual or pay for time away.
  Contractors are paid under their agreement and interns are unpaid, as
  [engagement types](../engagement-types/) and
  [internships](../internships/) say.
- Where mandatory local law gives you a right to time away (for example for
  illness or a protected reason), that law prevails over this page.
- Taking reasonable time away is not a violation and is never held against
  you.

### Unplanned absence

If you are unwell or an emergency stops you, tell your engagement lead,
supervisor or team channel as soon as you reasonably can. You do not have to
share medical or personal details. If you choose to, they are handled under
the [personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) and shared
only with those who need to know.

### Keeping the work safe while you are away

- Hand over or flag open work, deadlines and client commitments before a
  planned absence.
- Never share a password or let someone use your account to cover for you:
  see the [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/). Ask for access to
  be granted to the person who will cover instead.
- During a client engagement, flag absences that touch the client's sprint
  or SLA early: see [client engagements](../../engineering/clients/).

### Long or unexplained absence

If you cannot be reached while work you committed to is waiting, the
engagement lead will try to contact you. Cuesoft may then reassign the work
and, where the risk requires it, suspend your access until you are back.
That is a protective step, not a finding of fault. Going silent on
committed work, with no word at all, is handled under
[work ethic](../../general/work-ethic/) and [violations](../violation/).

### Concerns, law and changes

If you think a decision about time away is unfair or discriminatory, report
it as [the policies page](../../policies/#reporting-a-concern) says. No one is
penalised for a good-faith report. Local law applies on top of this page, the
signed agreement prevails where they differ, and this page changes the way
the handbook does: see [changing a policy](../../policies/#changing-a-policy).
