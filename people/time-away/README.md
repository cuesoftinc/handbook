# Time Away

We are deliverable-based, not clock-based, so there is no leave ledger —
there is communication.

- **Contractors** manage their own schedules. What matters is the agreed
  scope and milestones; plan your time away so commitments hold, and tell
  the people your absence affects before it starts.
- **Interns** coordinate time away with their supervisor ahead of time, so
  sprint planning can account for it. Reasonable time away is never held
  against anyone — unannounced disappearance during committed work is a
  different matter (see [work ethic](../../general/work-ethic/)).
- **During client engagements**, absences that affect the client's sprint
  or SLA must be flagged to the engagement lead early enough to cover them
  — see [client engagements](../../engineering/clients/).

Rest is part of doing excellent work. Take it, plan it, communicate it.
