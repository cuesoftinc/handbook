# Working as a Contractor

Independent contractors are engaged for a defined scope of work under an
Independent Contractor Agreement (ICA). This page summarises how those
engagements run; the signed ICA always governs.

## The engagement

- **Scope is written.** The services, deliverables and timeline are stated
  in the agreement. Do not deviate from scope without prior written
  approval, and do not accept scope changes informally.
- **Milestone-based compensation.** The standard shape is a fixed fee paid
  against milestones, typically a portion on execution and the balance on
  completion, as stated in your agreement. Work commences on receipt of the
  initial payment.
- **Timelines are commitments.** The agreed timeline is adhered to; missing
  milestones without reasonable justification is a material breach of the
  agreement, not just a scheduling problem.

## Your responsibilities

- **Independence.** You are not an employee, agent or representative of
  Cuesoft, and you cannot bind the company to anything.
- **Taxes.** You are solely responsible for your own taxes (income tax,
  VAT, withholding and other statutory levies) on compensation received.
- **Equipment.** You provide and maintain your own equipment, software and
  tools.
- **Expenses.** Reasonable, necessary expenses are reimbursable **only**
  when pre-approved in writing; submit expense reports with documentation
  within 3 to 5 days of incurring them.
- **Confidentiality and data.** The
  [confidentiality policy](../../policies/confidentiality/) applies in full,
  and personal data you touch must be handled per the
  [data-protection policy](../../policies/data-protection/).

## Intellectual property

Everything created in connection with the services (designs, code,
documentation, inventions) is assigned to Cuesoft (or, on client work, to
the client per that engagement's terms). See the
[intellectual property policy](../../policies/intellectual-property/).

## Ending an engagement

Either side may terminate with 30 days' written notice (or as your
agreement states). On termination: stop work, hand over all work product and
company materials, and Cuesoft pays for services rendered to the date of
termination.

## More detail

### Status and authority

- No employment relationship, partnership, joint venture or agency is
  created by the agreement, this handbook or the way the work is done.
  You control how and where you do the work, within the agreed scope and
  timeline, and the policies that protect clients, data and people.
- You have no authority to sign, promise or pay anything for Cuesoft, speak
  for it, or take on liabilities in its name. Only the founders can.
- Employment benefits, leave and insurance that employees may have are not
  part of an independent engagement, unless your signed agreement says so.
- Your taxes are your own, as set out under your responsibilities above.

If we have no tax identification number on file for you, we withhold 10% of
what we owe you and remit it: see the
[personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/).
Cuesoft withholds tax only where Nigerian law requires it for a contractor in
Nigeria, and says so on the payment.

### Confidentiality, data and security

- The [confidentiality policy](../../policies/confidentiality/) covers all
  non-public information you meet, including client information, and
  survives the engagement.
- Handle personal data under the
  [data-protection policy](../../policies/data-protection/), with the
  access you need and no more.
- Use company and client accounts under the
  [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/) and your own equipment
  under the [BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/). Report a lost device, a
  lost credential or a suspected breach at once to
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).
- Terms on client work, including the client's own confidentiality, apply
  on top: see [client engagements](../../engineering/clients/).

### Intellectual property

The assignment above is the rule: see the
[intellectual property policy](../../policies/intellectual-property/) and
the public [intellectual property terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/legal/intellectual-property/).
Do not bring in code, designs, data or materials you do not have the right
to use, and tell the engagement lead if a deliverable includes third-party
or open-source material, with its licence.

### Conflicts of interest and outside work

- Tell the engagement lead or a founder **before** you take on anything that
  conflicts, or could look like it conflicts, with your work for Cuesoft or
  a Cuesoft client: for example work for a direct competitor of the client
  on the same product, a financial interest in a supplier we are choosing, or
  a personal relationship with a person deciding on your work.
- Do not use Cuesoft or client confidential information, accounts, time or
  equipment for outside work.
- Do not accept gifts, payments or favours from a client, supplier or
  candidate in connection with Cuesoft work without telling a founder.
- Declaring a conflict is never a violation. Not declaring one can be.
- This page does not otherwise limit what you do outside the engagement.
- Outside work is allowed. Do not work for a direct competitor of Cuesoft
  without our written approval, and tell us about anything that could
  conflict with your work for Cuesoft.

### Non-solicitation

For **12 months** after your engagement ends, do not solicit, or try to take
away, a Cuesoft client, learner, intern or contractor you worked with during
the engagement. There is no non-compete: once the engagement ends, you may
work for anyone. Where your signed agreement says otherwise, it prevails.

The signed agreement governs any restriction on soliciting clients or
people. This page adds none.

### Equipment, expenses and property

Your equipment stays yours: see the [BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/). Any
property Cuesoft issues stays Cuesoft's, and is looked after as
[company assets](../company-assets/) says.

### Ending an engagement, in more detail

- Either side may end the engagement as the agreement says, and the
  agreement's notice, cure and termination terms prevail over this page. The
  [violations](../violation/) page says what happens on severe misconduct.
- Cuesoft may suspend your access and pause work while it gathers facts about
  a concern: see
  [what happens after a report](../../policies/#what-happens-after-a-report).
- On the last day, hand over all work product, documentation, credentials
  and materials, return company property, and remove company and client data
  from your devices and accounts: see
  [onboarding and offboarding](../onboarding-offboarding/).
- Confidentiality, intellectual property assignment, and any other clause
  the agreement says survives, continue after the engagement ends.
- Cuesoft pays for services rendered to the date of termination, as set out
  above, and the agreement says how an unfinished milestone is treated.

### Reporting, consequences, records, law and changes

Report a concern to [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO,
and if it concerns them to the CEO or another founder: see
[how to report a concern](../../policies/#reporting-a-concern). You are
protected from retaliation for a good-faith report. Breach is handled under
[violations](../violation/) and the agreement. Personal details and
engagement records follow the
[personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) and are kept as
the privacy policy's [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/)
says. Local law applies on top of this page, the signed ICA prevails where
they differ, and disputes follow the agreement.
