# Performance Reviews

Reviews exist to make work better and pathways real — not to ambush anyone.
Expectations are set at the start of a period; the review measures against
exactly those expectations, in writing.

## Interns

- **Cadence:** a mid-term review and an end-of-term review across the
  six-month internship, plus continuous feedback in code review and sprints.
- **Evaluated on:** quality of shipped work and attention to detail,
  timeliness against sprint commitments, collaboration and communication,
  initiative and growth — the ACE values, made measurable.
- **What it feeds:** the end-of-term review is the record that carries an
  intern forward — into a renewed term, a recommendation through CueHIRE™,
  or a clean, referenced exit. A public commit history plus a written
  review is a strong portfolio; neither is a promise of employment.
- **Reviewers:** the supervising founder (or designated lead) runs the
  review, gathers peer input where the work was collaborative, shares it in
  writing, and hears the intern's own reflection before it is filed.

## Contractors — milestone acceptance, not reviews

Contractors are independent professionals, so there is no performance
review; there is **acceptance of deliverables** against the signed
agreement. At each milestone and at engagement end, the engagement lead
records in writing whether the deliverable met the agreed scope, timeline
and handover quality. That record feeds renewal, extension or expansion of
engagements — based on delivered value, never favouritism.
