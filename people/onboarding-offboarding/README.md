# Onboarding and Offboarding

## Onboarding

Nothing starts before the agreement: sign your Independent Contractor or
Internship Agreement first, counter-signed by a founder. Then, in order:

1. **Email group.** You are added to the relevant `@cuesoft.io` group for
   your engagement; formal and sensitive correspondence lives on email.
2. **Your channel.** Contractors join Slack; CueLABS™ interns join the
   Discord community. Set your real name and a recognisable photo, and join
   the channels for your team and project.
3. **GitHub.** Send your username to the CTO to be added to the
   [cuesoftinc](https://github.com/cuesoftinc) organisation with the access
   your work needs — least privilege, per the
   [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/).
4. **Project tracker.** Client work lives on Linear (CueHIRE™); the
   open-source products live on GitHub Projects (CueLABS™). You will be
   invited to the tracker your work uses (see
   [project management](../../engineering/project-management/)).
5. **Read the handbook** — particularly [values](../../general/values/),
   your engagement page ([contractors](../contractors/) or
   [internships](../internships/)), and the [policies](../../policies/).

Never send anyone your password for any service — no legitimate Cuesoft
process asks for one.

## Offboarding

When an engagement ends (completion, expiry or termination):

- Access is revoked the same day: email group, Slack or Discord, Linear, GitHub, cloud
  consoles and any client systems.
- All work product, documentation and company materials are handed over
  before the final milestone is closed out.
- Company property, where issued, is returned per your agreement.
- Confidentiality and IP assignment survive the engagement — leaving does
  not end them.
