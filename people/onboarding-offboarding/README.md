# Onboarding and Offboarding

## Onboarding

Nothing starts before the agreement: sign your Independent Contractor or
Internship Agreement first, counter-signed by a founder. Then, in order:

1. **Email group.** You are added to the relevant `@cuesoft.io` group for
   your engagement; formal and sensitive correspondence lives on email.
2. **Your channel.** Contractors join Slack; CueLABS™ interns join the
   Discord community. Set your real name and a recognisable photo, and join
   the channels for your team and project.
3. **GitHub.** Send your username to the CTO to be added to the
   [cuesoftinc](https://github.com/cuesoftinc) organisation with the access
   your work needs: least privilege, per the
   [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/).
4. **Project tracker.** Client work lives on Linear (CueHIRE™); the
   open-source products live on GitHub Projects (CueLABS™). You will be
   invited to the tracker your work uses (see
   [project management](../../engineering/project-management/)).
5. **Read the handbook**: particularly [values](../../general/values/),
   your engagement page ([contractors](../contractors/) or
   [internships](../internships/)), and the [policies](../../policies/).

Never send anyone your password for any service: no legitimate Cuesoft
process asks for one.

## Offboarding

When an engagement ends (completion, expiry or termination):

- Access is revoked the same day: email group, Slack or Discord, Linear, GitHub, cloud
  consoles and any client systems.
- All work product, documentation and company materials are handed over
  before the final milestone is closed out.
- Company property, where issued, is returned per your agreement.
- Confidentiality and IP assignment survive the engagement: leaving does
  not end them.

## More detail

### Who this applies to

Everyone who joins or leaves: independent contractors, CueLABS™ interns and
anyone else given access on Cuesoft's behalf. The founders follow the same
steps for their own accounts.

### Before access is granted

- Access is granted only after the signed agreement, and is limited to what
  the work needs. Cuesoft may delay or refuse access until the agreement is
  signed and the details it needs are in.
- The details we ask you for, and why, are in the
  [personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/): read it
  before you send them.
- You are expected to read the handbook and its policies and to follow them
  from your first day: confidentiality, data protection, cybersecurity,
  BYOD, intellectual property and anti-harassment in particular.

### Accounts and acceptable use

- Accounts and credentials are personal to you and used only for your work:
  see the [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/) and
  [company assets](../company-assets/).
- Set up two-factor authentication on every company account on day one.

### Offboarding, in more detail

- **Same day.** Access is revoked the same day the engagement ends. Access
  can be suspended earlier while facts about a concern are gathered, or if
  a security risk is found.
- **Handover first.** All work product, documentation and company materials
  are handed over before access is removed, so nothing is lost. Do not
  delete work Cuesoft has not yet received.
- **Return and removal.** Return company property, then remove company and
  client data, credentials, tokens and company accounts from your own
  devices, browsers and personal accounts, and confirm in writing that you
  have: see the [BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/).
- **What stays.** Public contributions to the open-source products stay
  public. Confidentiality and intellectual property assignment survive.
  Nothing here asks you to delete your own personal records of your
  engagement, such as your agreement and payment records.
- **Final payment.** Any payment due is made as your agreement says.
- **Your records.** Personnel records are kept for the engagement and six
  years after it ends, as the
  [personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) says. You can
  ask for access or correction as the privacy policy's
  [rights page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/rights/your-rights/) explains.

### Leaving after a concern or violation

Where an engagement ends after a report or a violation, access is revoked
without waiting for the end date, and offboarding follows the same steps.
The right to respond and the other protections in
[violations](../violation/) still apply.

### Concerns, law and changes

Report a concern as
[the policies page](../../policies/#reporting-a-concern) says. Local law
applies on top of this page, the signed agreement prevails where they differ,
and this page changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../../policies/#changing-a-policy).
