# Company Assets

By default, contractors and interns work on their own equipment: see the
[BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/).

Where Cuesoft does issue property (a device, a licence, test hardware, or
access credentials), the terms are simple:

- It remains Cuesoft property, documented at handover.
- Faults are reported, not self-repaired: describe the problem to
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and await instructions.
- It is returned promptly when your engagement ends, per your agreement.
  Unreturned property is treated as what it is, and pursued accordingly.
- Company-provided access and credentials are personal to you, never
  shared, and cease with the engagement.

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to everyone who is given Cuesoft property: the founders,
contractors and interns. It covers every device, licence, test hardware,
account, credential and key issued in Cuesoft's name, and the data on them.

## Acceptable use

- Use company property and accounts only for the work they were issued for.
- Keep them safe, up to date and locked: see the
  [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/).
- Do not lend, sell, pledge, modify, sub-licence or take company property
  out of use without written approval, and do not install software that is
  not approved.
- Company licences and accounts stay in Cuesoft's name and cannot be
  transferred to you or anyone else.

## Monitoring

Company-issued devices and accounts belong to Cuesoft, so use them on the
footing that the company can review what is held in them.

## Lost, stolen or damaged property

Report loss, theft, damage or suspected misuse of company property
**immediately** to [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io). We will
revoke access and, where data was on it, treat it as an incident under the
[cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/) and the
[data-protection policy](../../policies/data-protection/). Prompt reporting
is not held against you. Ordinary wear is not a fault.

## Return and wiping at the end of an engagement

- Property is returned promptly when the engagement ends, or earlier on
  request, in the condition it was handed over apart from ordinary wear.
- Do not wipe or reset a device before you hand it over unless we ask you
  to. We remove company data and revoke access.
- Access, licences and credentials end with the engagement: see
  [onboarding and offboarding](../onboarding-offboarding/).

## Liability

You are responsible for the care of property you hold. Nothing here makes
you personally liable to Cuesoft beyond what your signed agreement and the
law provide, and Cuesoft's remedies for unreturned or misused property are
those of the agreement and the law.

## Reporting, consequences, records, law and changes

Report a concern about company property to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO, and if it concerns
them to the CEO or another founder, as
[the policies page](../../policies/) explains. No one is penalised for a
good-faith report, and a knowingly false one is a violation. Misuse is
handled under [violations](../violation/). Records are kept as the
[rules for every policy](../../policies/#records) say, and personal details
follow the [personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/).
Local law applies on top of this page, the signed agreement prevails where
they differ, and the page changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../../policies/#changing-a-policy).
