# Writing for The CueBlog™

The CueBlog™ ([blog.cuesoft.io](https://blog.cuesoft.io)) is the company's
publication, hosted on Medium. Anyone working with Cuesoft is encouraged to
write — engineering notes, lessons from live builds, product stories,
cohort experiences.

- Pitch your article to the CTO, who edits the publication: a title and
  three sentences on what it covers is enough.
- Write in the company voice: declarative, honest, no hype. Never disclose
  confidential or client-specific information — when in doubt, check the
  [confidentiality policy](../../policies/confidentiality/).
- Your byline is yours. Published articles credit you as the author, and
  they compound the same public record your commits do.
