# CueLABS™ Internships

CueLABS™ interns ship production code to live, MIT-licensed products
(Apparule, Expendit and Upstat) under the published engineering standard,
with a supervisor and a real review bar. The git log is the CV. Each
internship runs under a signed Internship Agreement; this page summarises
it, the agreement governs.

## The shape of an internship

- **Role-based.** Interns are engaged into a specific role (for example
  Backend Engineer) with defined duties: shipping features, reviewing code,
  collaborating across the team, documenting, and working agile ceremonies
  (sprint planning, standups).
- **Three or six months.** Internships run for a three (3) or six (6) month
  term from the commencement date, whichever the agreement states, ending
  automatically at term unless renewed.
- **Unpaid, data covered.** Internships are unpaid (no salary, wages or
  hourly compensation) and create no employment relationship. Cuesoft
  provides a monthly **data allowance of ₦20,000** to cover internet costs
  for remote work throughout the internship.
- **On Discord.** Intern day-to-day happens in the CueLABS™ Discord
  community: standup updates, cohort coordination and collaboration.
- **Supervised.** Every intern works under a designated supervisor, with
  feedback through reviews and the cadence in
  [performance reviews](../performance-review/).

## Obligations that come with it

- **Confidentiality (NDA).** The agreement carries a full NDA over source
  code, business plans, client information and everything else non-public.
  It survives the internship. See
  [confidentiality](../../policies/confidentiality/).
- **Intellectual property.** Work product created during the internship is
  assigned to Cuesoft. For the open-source products, Cuesoft publishes that
  work under MIT, which is how interns end up with a public, attributable
  commit history under their own GitHub identity. See
  [intellectual property](../../policies/intellectual-property/).
- **Ending it.** Either side may end the internship with seven (7) days'
  written notice. On exit, return company property and cease using
  company-provided access and credentials.

## After the internship

The pathway (learn at CueTA™, build at CueLABS™, go forward through
CueHIRE™) is real, and interns with strong records are recommended into
client engagements. Nothing here promises employment, a placement or a
salary; the record speaks and clients decide.

## More detail

### Who and what this covers

It covers CueLABS™ interns and researchers for the whole internship. The
public [CueLABS™ terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/divisions/cuelabs/) cover
applying and the programme. You must be at least 18 years old to apply.
The signed Internship Agreement prevails over this page and those terms
wherever they differ.

### Status

- An internship is unpaid and creates no employment relationship, no wages,
  benefits or leave entitlement, in any jurisdiction. You are not an agent
  or representative of Cuesoft and cannot bind it.
- The monthly data allowance is a contribution to connectivity cost, as the
  agreement states. It is not pay.

### Confidentiality, data and security

- The [confidentiality policy](../../policies/confidentiality/) applies in
  full, including to code, plans and client information, and survives the
  internship.
- Interns work in the open: the products are public and your commits carry
  your own GitHub identity. Never commit secrets, keys, `.env` files or
  anyone's personal data. A mistake is reported at once to
  [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) so the credential can be
  rotated.
- Follow the [cybersecurity policy](../../policies/cyber/),
  [BYOD policy](../../policies/byod/) and
  [data-protection policy](../../policies/data-protection/). Report a lost
  device or credential, or a suspected breach, immediately.
- Discord and other community channels are covered by the
  [communication policy](../../policies/communication/). The
  [anti-harassment policy](../../general/anti-harassment/) applies there
  and everywhere else you represent CueLABS™.

### Intellectual property

Work product you create in the internship is assigned to Cuesoft, and for
the open-source products Cuesoft publishes it under the MIT licence, with
your authorship in the public history. Work you create outside the
internship's scope, on your own time and equipment, is not assigned. Do not
bring in code, designs or data you have no right to use, and do not copy
from other employers, universities or clients. See the
[intellectual property policy](../../policies/intellectual-property/) and the
[CueLABS™ terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/divisions/cuelabs/).

### Conflicts of interest and outside work

Tell your supervisor before you take on anything that conflicts, or could
look like it conflicts, with your internship: for example work for a
competitor of a product you are shipping, or a personal relationship with a
person deciding on your work. Do not use Cuesoft or client confidential
information for outside work. Declaring a conflict is never a violation.

### Ending an internship, in more detail

- The internship ends at the end of its term, or earlier by seven (7) days'
  written notice from either side, or immediately for severe misconduct
  under [violations](../violation/).
- Cuesoft may suspend your access while it gathers facts about a concern:
  see [what happens after a report](../../policies/#what-happens-after-a-report).
- On the last day, hand over all work, return property, remove company data
  from your devices and accounts: see
  [onboarding and offboarding](../onboarding-offboarding/). Confidentiality
  and IP assignment continue. Your public commits stay public.

### Concerns, consequences, records, law and changes

Report a concern to [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO,
and if it concerns them to the CEO or another founder: see
[how to report a concern](../../policies/#reporting-a-concern). You are
protected from retaliation for a good-faith report. Breaches follow the
[violations](../violation/) ladder. Personal details follow the
[personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) and are kept as
the privacy policy's [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/)
says. Local law applies on top of this page, the signed agreement prevails
where they differ, and the page changes the way the handbook does.
