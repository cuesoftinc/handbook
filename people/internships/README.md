# CueLABS™ Internships

CueLABS™ interns ship production code to live, MIT-licensed products —
Apparule, Expendit and Upstat — under the published engineering standard,
with a supervisor and a real review bar. The git log is the CV. Each
internship runs under a signed Internship Agreement; this page summarises
it, the agreement governs.

## The shape of an internship

- **Role-based.** Interns are engaged into a specific role (for example
  Backend Engineer) with defined duties: shipping features, reviewing code,
  collaborating across the team, documenting, and working agile ceremonies
  (sprint planning, standups).
- **Six months.** The standard term is six (6) months from the commencement
  date, ending automatically at term unless renewed.
- **Unpaid, data covered.** Internships are unpaid — no salary, wages or
  hourly compensation — and create no employment relationship. Cuesoft
  provides a monthly **data allowance of ₦20,000** to cover internet costs
  for remote work throughout the internship.
- **On Discord.** Intern day-to-day happens in the CueLABS™ Discord
  community — standup updates, cohort coordination and collaboration.
- **Supervised.** Every intern works under a designated supervisor, with
  feedback through reviews and the cadence in
  [performance reviews](../performance-review/).

## Obligations that come with it

- **Confidentiality (NDA).** The agreement carries a full NDA over source
  code, business plans, client information and everything else non-public.
  It survives the internship. See
  [confidentiality](../../policies/confidentiality/).
- **Intellectual property.** Work product created during the internship is
  assigned to Cuesoft. For the open-source products, Cuesoft publishes that
  work under MIT — which is how interns end up with a public, attributable
  commit history under their own GitHub identity. See
  [intellectual property](../../policies/intellectual-property/).
- **Ending it.** Either side may end the internship with seven (7) days'
  written notice. On exit, return company property and cease using
  company-provided access and credentials.

## After the internship

The pathway — learn at CueTA™, build at CueLABS™, go forward through
CueHIRE™ — is real, and interns with strong records are recommended into
client engagements. Nothing here promises employment, a placement or a
salary; the record speaks and clients decide.
