# Work Ethic

Be true to your word. That is the whole policy; everything else is
elaboration.

- If you commit to a standup, a review, a client meeting or a deadline —
  keep it, or tell the affected people **before** the moment passes, not
  after.
- Missing a client commitment without notice damages more than your own
  standing; it spends trust the whole company earned.
- Consistent, communicated work beats heroic, unpredictable work. We would
  rather hear "this will slip two days, here is why" on Monday than silence
  until Friday.

Repeated broken commitments are handled under the
[violation](../../people/violation/) process.
