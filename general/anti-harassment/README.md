# Anti-Harassment Policy

## The short version

Sexual harassment, hate speech, discrimination, bullying and workplace
violence end your engagement with Cuesoft immediately. Where the conduct is
serious, we also report it to the appropriate law enforcement. If you are
the victim of or a witness to any of these, email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or report directly to the COO.
If the report concerns the COO or whoever reads that inbox, report directly
to the CEO or any other founder.

## Scope

This policy applies to everyone working with any Cuesoft entity: founders,
contractors and interns, in every location. Because we work remotely, most
interaction is calls, chat and written collaboration; the standard is the
same in every medium: contribute to an inclusive, collaborative environment
and respect each other at all times.

Local law applies on top of this policy. Incidents are investigated
promptly and discreetly, the affected person is kept informed, and where
local legal processes apply, they are followed.

## What happens on report

1. Report to the COO or [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io); a
   founder not involved in the incident handles it.
2. The facts are gathered from everyone involved, in confidence.
3. Action follows the finding, up to and including immediate termination
   of the engagement and, where warranted, referral to law enforcement.
   Harassment has no violation ladder and no second chance.

## What this policy means in plain words

These are working descriptions, not legal tests. Conduct can break this
policy without meeting a legal definition.

- **Harassment** is unwelcome conduct that offends, humiliates, intimidates
  or degrades someone, whether it happens once or repeatedly. Intent does
  not decide it: what matters is how a reasonable person would take it.
- **Sexual harassment** is unwelcome sexual attention or conduct: advances,
  requests for favours, sexual comments or jokes, sexual images or messages,
  unwanted touching, or treating a sexual response as a condition of work,
  pay, a reference or an opportunity.
- **Discrimination** is treating someone worse, or excluding them, because
  of who they are: for example their gender, race, colour, ethnic or
  national origin, marital status, age, disability, sexual orientation,
  gender identity, or political or religious belief (see
  [diversity, inclusion and equality](../../policies/diversity/)).
- **Bullying** is repeated unreasonable behaviour that undermines or
  isolates someone: public humiliation, constant belittling, deliberate
  exclusion from work, spreading rumours, or abusing seniority or access.
- **Hate speech** is language or imagery that attacks or demeans a person
  or group because of who they are, including jokes presented as banter.
- **Workplace violence** is any threat, intimidation or physical harm, and
  includes threats made by message, voice or video, and threats to a
  person's family, property or livelihood.

## Where it applies

- Calls, video meetings, chat (Slack and Discord), email, WhatsApp groups,
  code review comments, issues, pull requests, commit messages and shared
  documents.
- Client sites, client meetings, events, meetups, trips and social
  gatherings connected to Cuesoft, in person or online.
- Conduct outside work that affects a colleague's safety or ability to work
  with us, or that damages Cuesoft's reputation (see
  [values](../values/)).

## Clients, learners and other third parties

- The policy covers conduct **by** anyone working with Cuesoft **towards** a
  client, a learner, an applicant, a community member or any other third
  party.
- It also covers conduct **towards** anyone working with Cuesoft **by** a
  client, a learner or any other third party. You are not expected to put up
  with it because it comes from a client.
- Report it the same way. We may raise it with the client or third party,
  pause or end work with them, or remove you from that work. Being removed
  from work because you reported conduct is never counted against you.

## Non-retaliation

Nobody is penalised for reporting in good faith, for supporting someone who
reports, or for giving evidence. Retaliation is harassment in its own right
and is handled the same way: no ladder and no second chance. A report that
is made in good faith but not proven carries no consequence for the person
who made it.

## False or malicious reports

A report we find was knowingly false, or made to harm someone, is dishonesty
and is handled under [violations](../../people/violation/).

## Confidentiality of reports

- Reports, the identity of the person reporting and the evidence are shared
  only with those who need to know to investigate, act or meet a legal
  duty.
- The person reported is told what is alleged so they can respond. We do
  not promise to keep a name secret where fairness or the law requires it
  to be disclosed, and we say so before it happens.
- Everyone involved is asked to keep the matter confidential. Breaking that
  confidence can itself be a violation.

## Support for the person affected

- We take the report seriously, tell you what is happening and what to
  expect, and keep you informed of progress and the outcome as far as the
  confidentiality of others allows.
- We can take interim measures to protect you while we gather the facts:
  changing who you work with, pausing contact with a person or a client, or
  suspending the access of the person reported.
- You may go to the police, a regulator or a court at any point. We do not
  need to finish first, and we will cooperate.

## Interim measures

While facts are gathered, a founder who is not involved may suspend the
access of the person reported to accounts, repositories, channels and client
systems, and may pause their work. An interim measure is not a finding of
fault, and it does not wait for the investigation to finish.

## Right to respond

Before a decision is made, the person reported is told in writing what is
alleged and is given a fair chance to respond. This does not delay interim
measures, and it does not mean a report is shared with the person reported
in a way that would put the person who reported at risk.

## Consequences

Harassment is severe misconduct under [violations](../../people/violation/):
immediate termination of the engagement and, where warranted, referral to
law enforcement. This applies to founders, contractors and interns alike. It
sits alongside, and does not limit, the remedies of a signed agreement or
the law.

## Records

Reports, findings and outcomes are recorded in writing and kept
confidentially. Personal details in them are handled under the
[personnel privacy notice](../../policies/personnel-privacy/) and kept for
the periods in the privacy policy's
[retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/).

## Local law and the signed agreement

Local law applies on top of this policy, and where it gives you a protection
or a right, that prevails over anything here that would reduce it. Where
this policy and your signed agreement differ, the signed agreement wins. See
also the [rules that apply to every policy](../../policies/).

## Changing this policy

It changes the way the whole handbook changes: by pull request, approved and
merged by a founder. See
[changing a policy](../../policies/#changing-a-policy).
