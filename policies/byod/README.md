# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

We are a remote company and, per the contractor and internship agreements,
you provide your own working equipment: computer, phone, and whatever your
craft requires. Cuesoft does not provide devices, fuel or power allowances;
interns receive the monthly **data allowance** stated in their agreement
for connectivity.

What your own device must meet to touch company or client work:

- Up-to-date operating system and security patches.
- Full-disk encryption and a lock screen.
- The [cybersecurity policy](../cyber/) in full: 2FA on accounts, password
  manager, no company data in personal cloud storage.
- A reliable internet connection and power arrangement: your commitments
  in sprints assume both.

Where Cuesoft exceptionally issues equipment, the
[company assets](../../people/company-assets/) page governs it.

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to everyone who uses their own phone, computer, tablet, storage
or accounts to touch company or client data or systems: the founders,
contractors and interns. It covers the device, the software on it, the
networks it uses and every account or copy of company or client data on it.

## Acceptable use of your own device

- Use company and client accounts and data only for the work they were given
  for, and only through approved company systems.
- Keep work separate from personal use where you can: a separate browser
  profile or user account for work is the simplest way. Other people who use
  your device, including family, never see company or client data.
- Never keep copies of company or client data in personal cloud storage,
  personal email, personal messaging, USB drives or AI tools that Cuesoft
  has not approved: see the [cybersecurity policy](../cyber/).
- Do not remove or weaken the security settings the policy requires
  (patches, disk encryption, lock screen, two-factor authentication).
- Do not use pirated or unlicensed software to do company work.

## Monitoring

This policy does not authorise Cuesoft to look into your personal device,
your personal accounts or your personal messages. Company accounts and the
data in them belong to Cuesoft, so use them on the footing that the company
can review what is held in them.

## Lost, stolen or compromised devices

Report a lost, stolen or compromised device, or a device that held company
or client data and was shared, repaired or sold, **immediately** to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io), even if you are not sure data
was exposed. We will revoke access to company and client systems. A device
that held personal data also falls under the
[data-protection policy](../data-protection/), whose breach clock starts
when you find out.

## Returning and removing company data

When your engagement ends, or earlier if we ask:

- Hand over all work product and company materials first: see
  [onboarding and offboarding](../../people/onboarding-offboarding/).
- Then remove company and client data, credentials, tokens and company
  accounts from your devices, browsers and personal accounts, and tell us
  in writing that you have. Do not wipe data we have not yet received.
- Public contributions you made to the open-source products stay public.
  Nothing in this policy asks you to delete them.
- Confidentiality continues after you have removed the data: see the
  [confidentiality policy](../confidentiality/).

## Responsibility for your own device

You are responsible for your own device, its software and its repair. Cuesoft
is not responsible for loss of, damage to, or the cost of securing your own
device or your personal data on it, except as your signed agreement or the
law says. Nothing here makes you personally liable to Cuesoft beyond what
your signed agreement and the law provide.

## Reporting a concern, consequences and records

Report concerns, and anything you see that breaks this policy, as
[the policies page](../) explains: to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO, and if it concerns
them to the CEO or another founder. No one is penalised for a good-faith
report, and a knowingly false one is a violation. Breaking this policy is
handled under [violations](../../people/violation/), and careless handling
of company data is one of its named examples. Records of reports are kept as
the [rules for every policy](../#records) say, and your personal details
follow the [personnel privacy notice](../personnel-privacy/).

## Local law, signed agreements and changes

Local law applies on top of this policy, and the signed agreement prevails
where they differ. The policy changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
