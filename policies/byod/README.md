# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

We are a remote company and, per the contractor and internship agreements,
you provide your own working equipment: computer, phone, and whatever your
craft requires. Cuesoft does not provide devices, fuel or power allowances;
interns receive the monthly **data allowance** stated in their agreement
for connectivity.

What your own device must meet to touch company or client work:

- Up-to-date operating system and security patches.
- Full-disk encryption and a lock screen.
- The [cybersecurity policy](../cyber/) in full: 2FA on accounts, password
  manager, no company data in personal cloud storage.
- A reliable internet connection and power arrangement — your commitments
  in sprints assume both.

Where Cuesoft exceptionally issues equipment, the
[company assets](../../people/company-assets/) page governs it.
