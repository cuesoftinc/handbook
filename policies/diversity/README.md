# Diversity, Inclusion and Equality

We maintain a free, fair and credible working environment where people of
different backgrounds contribute meaningfully to what we build.

- We engage, renew and reward people based on capability and delivered
  work, nothing else.
- Gender, race, colour, ethnic or national origin, marital status, family
  circumstances, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, and
  political or religious belief are not factors in any engagement decision,
  and are never relevant to how anyone is treated here.
- Everyone is treated with dignity, courtesy and respect: colleagues,
  clients, applicants and community members alike.
- Accessibility is one of our IMPACT values, and it applies to how we work,
  not just what we ship.

Violations of this policy are handled under the
[anti-harassment policy](../../general/anti-harassment/) and the
[violation process](../../people/violation/).

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to everyone who works with or acts for Cuesoft: the founders,
contractors and interns. It covers every decision and every interaction:
whom we engage, renew, review, pay, recommend or end an engagement with,
and how people treat colleagues, clients, applicants, learners and community
members, in every medium.

## The rule

- No one is treated worse because of a characteristic in the list above,
  or any other protected characteristic under the local law that applies.
- We make reasonable adjustments, and ask what is needed, so that people
  with disabilities and other needs can take part and do their best work.
- Decisions on engagement, renewal, review and recommendation rest on the
  agreed expectations and the work, in writing: see
  [performance reviews](../../people/performance-review/).
- Nobody may instruct someone else to discriminate.

## Reporting a concern

Report discrimination, unequal treatment, or a barrier to taking part to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO, and if it concerns
them to the CEO or another founder: see
[how to report a concern](../#reporting-a-concern). Conduct that is also
harassment is handled under the
[anti-harassment policy](../../general/anti-harassment/).

## After a report, non-retaliation and false reports

Reports are handled as [the policies page](../#what-happens-after-a-report)
says: a founder who is not involved gathers the facts in confidence, may
take interim measures such as pausing a decision or suspending access, and
the person reported is told what is alleged and can respond. Nobody is
penalised for a good-faith report, and retaliation is a violation. A report
we find was knowingly false or malicious is dishonesty, handled under
[violations](../../people/violation/).

## Consequences and records

A breach of this policy is a violation: see
[violations](../../people/violation/). Records are kept confidentially, as the
[rules for every policy](../#records) say, and personal details follow the
[personnel privacy notice](../personnel-privacy/).

## Local law, signed agreements and changes

Local law applies on top of this policy, and where it gives you more
protection, that prevails. The signed agreement prevails where it differs.
The policy changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
