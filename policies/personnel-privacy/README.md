# Personnel Privacy Notice

This is the notice we give the people who work with us when we ask them for
personal details. [Data Protection](../data-protection/) is the internal duty
of care that binds everyone handling this data; this page is what the person
handing it over is entitled to be told.

Show it, or link to it, wherever the details are collected. A KYC form that
asks for a bank account and a next of kin without telling someone why is not
a form we should be sending.

## Who holds your data

Cuesoft Nigeria Limited (Ikoyi, Lagos) and Cuesoft Inc. (Dover, Delaware).
Reach us at [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

## What we collect

Your name, date of birth, personal phone number, email address, home address,
state and country, your tax identification number, a passport photograph, your
next of kin's name, relationship to you and phone number, and your bank account
details.

We do not ask for your NIN, your passport number, or a scan of any government
ID.

## Why we collect it

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Bank details, name | To pay you. This is performance of our engagement with you. |
| Identity and engagement dates | To meet tax and company-law record-keeping obligations. |
| Tax identification number | So we can pay you in full. Without a TIN on file we are required to withhold 10% of what we owe you and remit it, so giving us one means more of your money reaches you. |
| Home address, phone, email | To reach you and to send anything that has to go somewhere physical. |
| Passport photograph | Identification on internal systems and ID cards. |
| Next of kin | To reach someone if something happens to you while you are engaged with us. Nothing else. |

Next of kin details are the one item here collected for someone else's
benefit rather than ours. We do not contact them for any other reason, and we
do not pass them outside Cuesoft.

## Who sees it

- **Airtable** stores these records. Its servers are in the United States.
- **Your bank, and our accounting records in QuickBooks**, receive what is
  needed to make and account for payment.
- **Brevo** carries email we send you.

Nobody outside Cuesoft receives your next of kin details.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you are engaged with us, and for **six years** after the
engagement ends. That is the record-keeping window Nigerian tax and company
law set.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about
you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where we have no
obligation to keep it. Email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io). Requests are handled manually,
within the timelines the [privacy policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io)
commits to.

## Interns

Your data stipend is processed the same way as any other payment, so
everything above applies to you in the same terms.
