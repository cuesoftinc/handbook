# Personnel Privacy Notice

This is the notice we give the people who work with us when we ask them for
personal details. [Data Protection](../data-protection/) is the internal duty
of care that binds everyone handling this data; this page is what the person
handing it over is entitled to be told.

Show it, or link to it, wherever the details are collected. A KYC form that
asks for a bank account and a next of kin without telling someone why is not
a form we should be sending.

## Who holds your data

Cuesoft Nigeria Limited (Ikoyi, Lagos) and Cuesoft Inc. (Dover, Delaware).
Reach us at [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

## What we collect

Your name, date of birth, personal phone number, email address, home address,
state and country, your tax identification number, a passport photograph, your
next of kin's name, relationship to you and phone number, and your bank account
details.

We do not ask for your NIN, your passport number, or a scan of any government
ID.

## Why we collect it

| What                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Bank details, name            | To pay you. This is performance of our engagement with you.                                                                                                              |
| Identity and engagement dates | To meet tax and company-law record-keeping obligations.                                                                                                                  |
| Tax identification number     | So we can pay you in full. Without a TIN on file we are required to withhold 10% of what we owe you and remit it, so giving us one means more of your money reaches you. |
| Home address, phone, email    | To reach you and to send anything that has to go somewhere physical.                                                                                                     |
| Passport photograph           | Identification on internal systems and ID cards.                                                                                                                         |
| Next of kin                   | To reach someone if something happens to you while you are engaged with us. Nothing else.                                                                                |

Next of kin details are the one item here collected for someone else's
benefit rather than ours. We do not contact them for any other reason, and we
do not pass them outside Cuesoft.

## Who sees it

- **Airtable** stores these records. Its servers are in the United States.
- **Your bank, and our accounting records in QuickBooks**, receive what is
  needed to make and account for payment.
- **Brevo** carries email we send you.

Nobody outside Cuesoft receives your next of kin details.

## How long we keep it

For as long as you are engaged with us, and for **six years** after the
engagement ends. That is the record-keeping window Nigerian tax and company
law set.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about
you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where we have no
obligation to keep it. Email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io). Requests are handled manually,
within the timelines the [privacy policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io)
commits to.

## Interns

Your data stipend is processed the same way as any other payment, so
everything above applies to you in the same terms.

## Who this notice applies to

It applies to the founders, contractors and interns whose details Cuesoft
Nigeria Limited and Cuesoft Inc. collect, and to applicants for those
engagements. The public [privacy policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io) covers
everything else, and this notice sits beside it for personnel data.

## Why the law lets us use it

We rely on the bases the table above names: performance of our engagement
with you, and legal and tax obligations. Where we use any detail for a
different purpose, we tell you first. If we ever rely on your consent for
something, the form says so, it is optional, and you can withdraw it at any
time without affecting earlier lawful use. The privacy policy sets out these
bases for the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and the GDPR where it
applies, on its [Nigeria](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/jurisdictions/nigeria/)
and [EU/UK](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/jurisdictions/eu-uk/) pages.

## What you should tell us and what we expect of you

- Give us accurate details and tell us when they change.
- Your next of kin is a third party. Tell them you gave us their name and
  number and what we use it for.
- Do not send us anyone else's personal data, or sensitive data we did not
  ask for (the list above is what we ask for).

## Who can see it inside Cuesoft

Only people who need it for payment, record keeping or the purpose in the
table, under the [data-protection policy](../data-protection/): need to know,
approved systems only, and no copies in chat, personal cloud accounts or
tools Cuesoft has not approved. A breach is reported at once to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io), and the privacy policy's
[breach notification page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/breach/) says
what we do next.

## Keeping and deleting it

The period above is the six years after the engagement ends. The privacy
policy's [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/)
carries the same rule. Where a legal duty or a dispute requires us to keep a
record longer, we keep only what it needs, for as long as it needs.

## More about your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and
portability, as the privacy policy's
[rights page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/rights/your-rights/) explains: email
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io), and we verify who you are
first. We answer within one month. We make no solely automated decisions
about you with legal or similarly significant effect. If we refuse a request,
we say why. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission
or, where the GDPR applies, to your own supervisory authority.

## If something goes wrong

If we learn your personal data has been exposed, we assess it, contain it and
notify the authorities and you where the law requires, as the
[breach notification page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/breach/) says.

## Records of concerns about you

Where a concern about your conduct is reported, the report, your response and
the outcome are recorded and kept as the
[violations](../../people/violation/) page and the
[retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/) say. You can
ask to see what is recorded about you.

## Local law, signed agreements and changes

Local law applies on top of this notice. Where your signed agreement says
something different about your data, the agreement wins. We change this
notice the way we change the handbook: see
[changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
