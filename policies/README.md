## Rules that apply to every policy

The short version of each policy stays at the top of its page. The detail
below the short version, and the rules on this page, apply to every policy
in this handbook and to the
[anti-harassment policy](../general/anti-harassment/).

### Who the policies apply to

- Everyone who works with or acts for Cuesoft Nigeria Limited or Cuesoft
  Inc.: the founders, independent contractors and CueLABS™ interns, in
  every location.
- Anyone else who is given access to Cuesoft or client systems, data or
  channels, for as long as they have that access.
- In every medium: calls, chat, email, written collaboration, code review,
  client sites, events and anything done on Cuesoft's behalf.
- The policies do not make anyone an employee, agent or representative of
  Cuesoft: see [engagement types](../people/engagement-types/).

### Reporting a concern

- Email [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or report directly to
  the COO. Say what happened, when, where, who was involved and what
  evidence exists. Do not post it in a public or team channel.
- If the concern is about the COO, or about whoever reads that inbox,
  report directly to the CEO or any other founder.
- If the concern involves a client, a learner or another third party,
  report it the same way. You do not have to settle it with them first.
- A report made in good faith is welcome even if it turns out to be
  mistaken. Reporting a concern to Cuesoft never stops you from also
  telling the police, a regulator or a court.

### What happens after a report

1. The report is handled by a founder who is not involved in it.
2. The facts are gathered from everyone involved, in confidence, and the
   person reported is told what is alleged.
3. While the facts are gathered, we may take interim measures to protect
   people, clients and systems: suspending access to accounts, repositories
   and channels, pausing work, or changing who works with whom. An interim
   measure is not a finding and is not a penalty.
4. A founder who is not involved decides the outcome in writing, and the
   people involved are told the outcome as far as confidentiality allows.
5. Reports are kept confidential and shared only with people who need to
   know to investigate, act, or meet a legal duty.

### Non-retaliation

Nobody is penalised for reporting a concern in good faith, for helping an
investigation, or for refusing to do something that breaks a policy.
Retaliation, including threats, exclusion, ending an engagement or
withholding work because of a report, is itself a violation, and is handled
under [violations](../people/violation/).

### False or malicious reports

A report made in good faith is protected even if it is not proven. A report
we find was knowingly false or made to harm someone is dishonesty, and is
handled under [violations](../people/violation/).

### Consequences

Breaking a policy is a violation. What follows is set out in
[violations](../people/violation/): the written-warning ladder for interns,
the notice and cure terms of the signed agreement for contractors and
clients, and immediate termination for severe misconduct. Where the conduct
may be unlawful, we may also refer it to law enforcement or a regulator.

### Records

Reports, findings and outcomes are recorded in writing and kept
confidentially. Personal details inside them are handled under the
[personnel privacy notice](personnel-privacy/) and the
[data protection policy](data-protection/), and are kept for the periods
in the privacy policy's
[retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/).

### Local law and the signed agreement

- Local law applies on top of these policies. Where mandatory law gives
  you a protection or a right, it prevails over anything here that would
  reduce it.
- Where these policies and your signed agreement differ, the signed
  agreement wins, as the [handbook's main page](../) says.
- Nothing in these policies limits a right or remedy Cuesoft or you hold
  under a signed agreement or the law.
- Disputes under a signed agreement follow that agreement. For the
  Websites, see the terms for
  [Nigeria](https://terms.cuesoft.io/jurisdictions/nigeria/) and the
  [United States](https://terms.cuesoft.io/jurisdictions/united-states/).

### Changing a policy

Policies change the way the handbook changes: anyone working with Cuesoft
may propose a change by pull request, and a founder approves and merges it.
Every page shows when it last changed, and the public git history shows
what changed. A change never reduces what a signed agreement gives you.

### A note on length
