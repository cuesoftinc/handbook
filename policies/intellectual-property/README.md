# Intellectual Property

Who owns what, in one page. The signed agreement for your engagement is
always the authority; this is the map of how the pieces fit.

## Work you do for Cuesoft

Work product created in connection with a Cuesoft engagement — code,
designs, inventions, documentation, improvements — is **assigned to
Cuesoft**: all rights, title and interest, including copyright, patent and
trade-secret rights. This is stated in every contractor and internship
agreement, and the assignment survives the engagement.

## The open-source products

For Apparule, Expendit and Upstat, Cuesoft publishes the assigned work
under the **MIT licence** in public repositories. That is the deliberate
other half of the assignment: contributors' commits ship under their own
GitHub identities, so interns and engineers leave with a public,
attributable record of everything they built — the git log is the CV.

## Client work

On CueHIRE™ engagements, intellectual property in the deliverables created
specifically for a client belongs (or transfers) to that client per the
engagement's terms — typically on full payment. Cuesoft retains its
pre-existing tools, frameworks, templates, generic know-how and reusable
libraries.

## Cueprise™ and company platforms

Cueprise™ — its software, source code, architecture, interfaces and
documentation — is and remains **exclusively Cuesoft's property**.
Licensees receive a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use
it; configurations and integrations done for a licensee do not transfer
ownership. Licensee data inside Cueprise™, however, remains **the
licensee's property** — see [data protection](../data-protection/).
