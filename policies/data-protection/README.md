# Data Protection

Cuesoft handles personal data under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act
(NDPA) 2023** and NDPR 2019, the **EU/UK GDPR** where they apply, and
applicable **US state privacy laws**. The public-facing commitments live in
the [privacy policy](https://privacy.cuesoft.io); this page is the internal
duty of care for everyone who works with us.

## The rules of the road

- **Minimum necessary.** Collect and access only the personal data your
  work actually needs. Applicant, learner and client data is looked at for
  the task at hand, never browsed.
- **Approved systems only.** Personal data lives in the company's approved
  systems, never in personal cloud accounts, personal devices' local
  copies, or chat threads. Exports are the exception, approved in writing.
- **Client and licensee data is theirs.** On client engagements and
  Cueprise™ deployments, the data belongs to the client or licensee. We
  access it only for maintenance, support and issue resolution, nothing
  else, per the engagement's terms.
- **No secrets in chat.** Credentials and personal data never travel
  through WhatsApp or other informal channels: formal email or the
  approved secret manager, always (see [cybersecurity](../cyber/)).
- **Contractors carry the duty too.** Contractor agreements bind you to
  applicable data-protection law (including GDPR where it applies): handle
  personal data securely and lawfully.

## When something goes wrong

Report any suspected personal-data breach (loss, unauthorised access,
accidental disclosure) **immediately** to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and your engagement lead. Speed
matters legally: Nigerian law requires reporting qualifying breaches to the
Nigeria Data Protection Commission within **72 hours**, and affected people
must be informed where their rights are at risk. Your fast report is what
makes the company's lawful response possible.

## Rights requests

If anyone asks you about their personal data (access, correction,
deletion), do not handle it ad hoc. Forward the request to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) the same day; the privacy
policy commits us to answer within statutory timelines.

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to the founders, contractors, interns and anyone else who handles
personal data while acting for Cuesoft Nigeria Limited or Cuesoft Inc. It
covers personal data of every kind and in every form: applicants, learners,
clients' and licensees' users, colleagues, contacts and the people in test
or sample data.

## The rules, in more detail

- **Need to know.** Access to personal data is by need to know, for the task
  in front of you. Do not look up, copy or share someone's data out of
  curiosity, and tell a founder if you have access you do not need.
- **No personal data in public places.** Never put personal data, or
  credentials, in a public repository, issue, pull request, commit, log,
  screenshot, gist or public document. Use synthetic data in tests, demos
  and screenshots.
- **No personal data in AI tools without approval.** Do not paste or upload
  personal data into any AI tool, assistant, transcription or note-taking
  service that Cuesoft has not approved in writing for that data. Approval
  is requested from [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) before use,
  not after.
- **Purpose.** Use personal data only for the purpose it was collected for.
  A new purpose needs a founder's approval first.
- **Accuracy and deletion.** Fix errors you find. Do not keep personal data
  longer than the work needs, and delete it as the
  [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/) and the
  engagement's terms say.
- **Sharing.** Personal data goes only to people and processors Cuesoft has
  approved. International transfers follow the privacy policy's
  [transfers page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/transfers/).
- **Children.** Take extra care with the data of anyone under 18. See the
  privacy policy's [children page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/rights/children/).
- **Clients and licensees.** Where Cuesoft processes data for a client or
  licensee, it does so on their instructions and the engagement's terms, and
  you do the same. Do not use that data for anything else.

## Reporting a breach at once

A suspected breach includes a lost device, a misdirected email, an account
someone else used, data left in a public place, a leaked credential and a
supplier incident. Report it **at once** to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) and your engagement lead, in
writing, with what you know. Do not wait to be sure, do not try to fix it
quietly, and do not contact the people affected or any regulator yourself:
Cuesoft decides who is told and when. The privacy policy's
[breach notification page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/breach/) says
what we then do, and its
[security page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/security/) says how we
protect data.

## After a report and non-retaliation

A founder who is not involved handles the report, gathers facts in
confidence, and may suspend access while it does: see
[what happens after a report](../#what-happens-after-a-report). A good-faith
report is never penalised, even if it was an honest mistake of your own, and
a knowingly false report is a violation.

## Consequences

Careless handling of personal data is a violation under
[violations](../../people/violation/). A deliberate misuse, such as selling,
leaking or snooping, is severe misconduct. Cuesoft may also be required to
report it to a regulator, and a person who breaks data-protection law can
face liability under that law.

## Records, law and changes

Reports are recorded and kept as the [rules for every policy](../#records)
say. Where more than one law applies (the NDPA 2023, NDPR 2019, the GDPR and
UK GDPR, US state laws), follow the strictest that applies to the person
whose data it is. Where mandatory law gives people a right or a protection,
it prevails over anything here that would reduce it. The signed agreement
prevails where it differs. The policy changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
