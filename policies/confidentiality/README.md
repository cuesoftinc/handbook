# Confidentiality & NDA

Every Cuesoft agreement (contractor and intern alike) carries
confidentiality obligations. This policy states them once, plainly.

## What is confidential

Any non-public information you receive or produce while working with us, in
any medium, including: source code of private repositories, algorithms and
technical documentation, business plans and strategies, financial data,
client information and client lists, contact details, trade secrets,
proprietary processes, project details, database structures, system
architecture, and marketing or business development plans.

If information is marked confidential, it is. If its nature or the
circumstances of disclosure make it obviously confidential, it is. If you
are unsure, ask **before** sharing, never after.

## Your obligations

- Hold confidential information in strict confidence and handle it with the
  same care we use ourselves.
- No disclosure to any third party (including family, friends and
  colleagues outside the engagement) without prior written consent.
- Use it only for the work it was shared for.
- Report any unauthorised use, disclosure or suspected breach immediately,
  in writing, to [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io).

## What is not confidential

Information that is public through no fault of yours, and information the
law requires you to disclose. Our open-source products are public by
design: their code is not confidential; the clients' and company's private
material around them is.

## Duration

Confidentiality obligations **survive the end of your engagement**. Leaving
Cuesoft ends your access, not your obligations.

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to the founders, contractors, interns and anyone else who comes
across Cuesoft's or a client's non-public information while acting for
Cuesoft. It covers information in any form and any place: documents, code,
chat, calls, screens, whiteboards, notebooks and memory.

## Need to know

- Access to confidential information is by need to know. Use only what your
  work needs, share only with people on the engagement who need it, and do
  not browse.
- If you are asked for information by someone outside the engagement,
  including a journalist, a client's competitor, a recruiter or a family
  member, send them to a founder and say nothing.
- Client information is also protected by the client's own terms with
  Cuesoft. Where those are stricter, follow them.
- Do not put confidential information in public repositories, public
  documents, personal cloud storage or AI tools that Cuesoft has not
  approved in writing: see the [cybersecurity policy](../cyber/).
- Do not use confidential information for your own benefit or for any
  other client, employer or project.

## What you may still say

- You may say what you work on and which products you helped build, as the
  public products and your public commits already show.
- Nothing here stops you from reporting unlawful conduct to the police, a
  regulator or a court, from speaking to a lawyer in confidence, or from
  exercising a right that mandatory law gives you. Where the law forces
  disclosure, give us prompt notice where the law allows, so we can protect
  the information.

## Ownership and return

Confidential information belongs to Cuesoft or the client, and your access to
it is a limited permission, not a licence. Keep no copies after the
engagement ends: hand over and remove it as
[onboarding and offboarding](../../people/onboarding-offboarding/) says.

## Reporting a breach

Report any unauthorised use or disclosure, or suspected leak, **at once** in
writing to [hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO, and if it
concerns them to the CEO or another founder. Include what, when, who and how.
If personal data is involved, the [data-protection policy](../data-protection/)
clock runs. Fast reporting helps us contain the damage, and no one is
penalised for a good-faith report. A knowingly false report is a violation.

## After a report

Cuesoft gathers the facts in confidence, may suspend access while it does,
and tells the person concerned what is alleged and gives them a fair chance
to respond: see
[what happens after a report](../#what-happens-after-a-report). Cuesoft may
act to stop or recover a disclosure, including by notifying the people
affected.

## Consequences

A breach of confidentiality is severe misconduct: immediate termination of
the engagement and, where warranted, referral to law enforcement, under
[violations](../../people/violation/). It sits alongside the remedies in the
signed agreement and the law, including an injunction and damages, and
does not limit them.

## Relationship to the signed agreement and the public terms

The signed agreement's confidentiality terms govern and prevail. The public
[confidentiality terms](https://terms.cuesoft.io/legal/confidentiality/) say
the same for people with no signed agreement. Local law applies on top of
this policy.

## Records, law and changes

Reports and findings are recorded in writing and kept as the
[rules for every policy](../#records) say, with personal details handled under
the [personnel privacy notice](../personnel-privacy/). The policy changes the
way the handbook does: see [changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
