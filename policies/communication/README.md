# Communication Policy

The rule is simple: discussion happens where the work lives, and the
channel matches the content.

| Channel                          | Use it for                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Slack**                        | Contractor and internal engineering day-to-day: team channels, sprint coordination, technical discussion.                                                                                          |
| **Discord**                      | The CueLABS™ community: intern day-to-day, cohort coordination and open-source collaboration.                                                                                                      |
| **Linear / GitHub Projects**     | The work itself: issues, status, decisions about specific tasks.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Email (`@cuesoft.io` groups)** | High-stakes communication: agreements, approvals, access requests, anything formal or sensitive. Per-engagement groups (e.g. `cuehire-<client>@cuesoft.io`) keep continuity when personnel change. |
| **WhatsApp**                     | CueTA™ learner communication, and discussion with client representatives in per-engagement groups: never credentials, never formal approvals.                                                      |
| **Meetings**                     | Standups, sprint planning, monthly client reviews, escalations, with notes written back to the channel where the work lives.                                                                       |

Expectations:

- Async by default; respect timezones between Lagos and the US.
- Respond within a reasonable working-day window; mark true urgencies as
  such, sparingly.
- Decisions made in calls or DMs get written into the tracker or channel:
  context trapped in a private thread is context lost.

## Who this applies to and what it covers

It applies to everyone who communicates for or about Cuesoft or its clients:
the founders, contractors and interns. It covers every channel in the table
above, and any other channel used for Cuesoft or client work.

## Rules for every channel

- **Conduct.** The [anti-harassment policy](../../general/anti-harassment/)
  and the [diversity policy](../diversity/) apply in every channel, public
  or private, and in every medium. Messages in a private chat count.
- **Confidentiality.** Do not post confidential or client information where
  people who should not see it can read it: see the
  [confidentiality policy](../confidentiality/). Check who is in a channel,
  group or call before you share.
- **No secrets.** Never send credentials, keys or personal data in chat,
  WhatsApp or screenshots: see the [cybersecurity policy](../cyber/) and
  [data protection](../data-protection/).
- **Recording and AI note-takers.** Do not record a call, or turn on an AI
  note-taker, that includes other people without telling them first, and do
  not feed a call's notes, transcripts or messages that hold confidential or
  personal data into an AI tool that Cuesoft has not approved.
- **Speaking for Cuesoft.** Do not speak or post publicly for Cuesoft, or
  sign anything on its behalf, unless you are a founder or a founder has
  asked you to. Your own writing is yours: see
  [writing for The CueBlog™](../../people/blogs/).
- **Clients and learners.** Keep communication with clients, learners and
  other third parties professional and in the channel the engagement uses.
  Do not move a client conversation to a personal account.

## Records and access

Formal communication is on email so there is a record. Company channels
(Slack, Discord, Linear, GitHub Projects, the `@cuesoft.io` groups) belong to
Cuesoft, and Cuesoft may keep the messages in them for as long as the work
needs and the law requires. Messages that hold personal data are kept as the
privacy policy's [retention page](https://privacy.cuesoft.io/handling/retention/)
says.

## Reporting a concern, consequences, law and changes

Report a communication that breaks this policy, or abuse in any channel, to
[hello@cuesoft.io](mailto:hello@cuesoft.io) or the COO, and if it concerns
them to the CEO or another founder: see
[how to report a concern](../#reporting-a-concern). No one is penalised for a
good-faith report, and a knowingly false one is a violation. Breaches are
handled under [violations](../../people/violation/). Local law applies on top
of this policy, the signed agreement prevails where they differ, and the
policy changes the way the handbook does: see
[changing a policy](../#changing-a-policy).
